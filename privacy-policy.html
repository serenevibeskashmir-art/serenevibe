Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

Serene Vibes Kashmir ("we", "us") is a travel agency based at NH-44, Lethpora Awantipora, Jammu & Kashmir 192122, India. This page explains what personal details we collect when you contact us or book a trip, why we collect them, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Details you give us when you enquire or book: your name, phone number, email address, travel dates, destinations, number of travellers, budget and special requests.
  • Messages you send us on WhatsApp, including your WhatsApp phone number and profile name, and any photos or documents you choose to share with us.
  • Messages you send to our website's travel assistant, so it can suggest a route and estimate.
  • Basic technical data your browser sends when you visit this website, such as device type and pages viewed.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry, prepare itineraries and quotations, and arrange hotels, transport and activities.
  • To keep a record of your enquiry so we can follow up and do not lose your request.
  • To confirm bookings and send trip details and updates.
  • To improve our website and service.

We do not sell your personal details, and we do not use them for advertising by other companies.

WhatsApp

When you message our business WhatsApp number, WhatsApp (Meta) delivers the message to us. We save the message text, your number and your profile name in our private booking records so our team can follow up. Details such as your destination, dates and number of travellers may be picked out of the chat automatically to fill in your enquiry. Meta's handling of your data is covered by WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

Who we share it with

We share only what is needed to deliver your trip, with the hotels, drivers, guides and activity providers who serve you, and with service providers who host our systems or send email on our behalf. We may disclose details if the law requires us to.

Payments

Payments are made by bank transfer or UPI directly through your bank or payment app. We do not store your card, UPI PIN or bank login details.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and booking records for as long as needed to serve you and to meet our accounting and legal duties, and then delete or anonymise them.

Security

Our records sit behind a password-protected admin area, and access is limited to our team. No online system is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your details.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the details we hold about you, correct them, or delete them. You can also tell us to stop contacting you. Write to us at the address below and we will respond promptly.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
