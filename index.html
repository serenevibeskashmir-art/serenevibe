Kashmir through the seasons

Each season changes what the valley has to offer — select one to see what to plan around.

  • Indira Gandhi Tulip Garden is in full bloom from late March through mid-April — Asia's largest tulip garden.
  • Orchard blossoms spread across the valley through April; ideal light for photography.
  • Mild days, cool evenings — pack layers rather than heavy winter wear.
Plan a Mar–May trip
  • Gulmarg's gondola runs to Apharwat Peak in clear alpine weather — the meadows are at their greenest.
  • Pahalgam's Lidder riverside walks and Betaab Valley are most accessible this season.
  • This is peak season — hotels and gondola slots fill up, so book 4–6 weeks ahead.
Plan a Jun–Aug trip
  • Chinar trees at Nishat Bagh and Shalimar Bagh turn deep amber through late September and October.
  • Noticeably fewer crowds than summer, with lower hotel rates across all five destinations.
  • Crisp, clear air gives the sharpest mountain views of the year — a favourite for photographers.
Plan a Sep–Nov trip
  • Gulmarg's slopes and gondola open for skiing and snowboarding through the coldest months.
  • Dal Lake's shallows can freeze in the coldest stretches of January — a rare sight for most visitors.
  • Srinagar hotel rates are at their lowest, with houseboats offering heated stays on the lake.
