When to go

Kashmir through
the seasons

Each season changes what the valley offers. Pick the one that suits what you want to see.

Mar – May

Spring Bloom

Tulip gardens open in Srinagar, orchards blossom across the valley, and Doodhpathri's meadows turn green.

Jun – Aug

Summer Greens

Peak season for Gulmarg's meadows, Pahalgam's riverside walks, and family sightseeing across all five stops.

Sep – Nov

Autumn Gold

Chinar trees turn amber across Srinagar's gardens, with fewer crowds, crisp air and dramatic valley light.

Dec – Feb

Winter Snow

Gulmarg becomes a ski destination under snow-laden slopes, while Srinagar settles into a quieter, frozen calm.
